    SEQUENTIAL OPENING GENERATOR

    Every sequence has its number.

    Exact, zero-based enumeration of legal move sequences. Canonical ordering: from square, to square, promotion suffix (UCI lexicographic order). Different sequences may transpose to the same position.

    Select a depth and rank.

    Strict exact random access is limited to depths 1–3 to protect mobile CPU and memory. No modulo selection, no wraparound, no balance filter. Computation runs in a separate worker with a 15-second / 250,000-node budget and a bounded subtree cache. Cancelling terminates the worker.
    

    MOTOR ARENA
    Stockfish vs Stockfish.
    Two independent, single-threaded engine workers, each with a 16 MB hash budget. Matches are archived and never change credits. Keep this page visible on mobile.
    Uses the selected Play clock, strength and atlas depth/rank. Each completed match advances to the next legal opening sequence; stops at the end without repeating.
    Pause/resume is available on the board. Reload restores the paused/running series and remaining games. Maximum two engine workers; the opening worker is finished before the engines start. Leela and Windows UCI bridges are future adapters. Fritz EXEs cannot run on GitHub Pages; do not upload proprietary engines.
